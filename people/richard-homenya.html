Richard Homenya

PhD Student, University of Chicago Booth School of Business

Richard is a Behavioral Science PhD student at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. He holds a Master of Science in Experimental Psychology from William & Mary and a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the University of Ghana.

Richard’s research interests lie at the intersection of cross-cultural psychology and socio-ecological psychology. He is particularly interested in investigating how cultural factors shape social behaviors. His research also explores the influence of subsistence patterns and ecological factors on social behavior across diverse cultures, as well as cross-cultural comparisons between regions like West Africa, East Asia, and North America.
