Undergraduates

PSY 2650R

Laboratory Research on Culture, Technology, and the Mind

2026 Fall · Full term · Section 001 · Class number 16747

Undergraduates join the lab through this course, working as research assistants for course credit. Each student pairs with a graduate student or postdoctoral fellow on an original question about how culture, technology, and the mind shape one another, using methods such as experiments, cross-cultural surveys, natural language processing, archival data, or computer simulations. Weekly lab meetings follow an idea from first sketch through to publication.

Senior honors theses

Harvard undergraduates hoping to write a senior thesis with the lab should tell us by January 15 of their junior year. Places are limited. Most years bring more requests than the lab can advise, so we are not able to say yes to everyone.

Preference goes to students already working in the lab as research assistants. If you have not worked with us before, it is worth arranging a conversation with Joshua Conrad Jackson well ahead of the deadline; that meeting carries real weight in the decision.

There is no formal application and nothing to prepare in advance. Email Joshua directly and describe what you would like to study.
